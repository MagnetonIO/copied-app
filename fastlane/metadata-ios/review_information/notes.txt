VERSION 1.3.4 (19)
This replacement build aligns the iOS and Mac release versions and fixes a crash during background iCloud sync by serializing sync metadata access. Existing clipping data is preserved; no library reset is required. The iOS app and both extensions use version 1.3.4 (19).

Copied is an iCloud-synced clipboard manager for iPhone and iPad. No sign-in required — sync uses the device's iCloud account automatically.

QUICK START
1. Launch the app — clipboard history (initially empty) shows on the main screen.
2. From any other app, use the system Share Sheet → "Save to Copied" to add a clipping. Alternatively, use the Action Extension "Copied Clipper" from the share sheet for one-tap capture.
3. Switch back to Copied — the new clipping appears at the top of the list.
4. Tap any clipping to copy it back to the system pasteboard, ready to paste anywhere.

iCloud SYNC IN-APP PURCHASE
Local clipboard history is free. iCloud Sync is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase: com.magneton.copied.icloud_sync ($4.99 USD; the app displays the localized App Store price).

To locate the purchase on iPhone or iPad:
1. Launch Copied and open the Copied clippings list.
2. On iPhone, tap the three-slider icon beside the clipping count at the bottom-right, just above Search. This opens Settings; the circular three-line button at the top-right is the content filter, not Settings. On iPad, tap Settings in the left sidebar below Trash; use the sidebar button at the top-left if the sidebar is hidden.
3. Tap the iCloud Sync row (it shows Disabled before purchase).
4. The Unlock iCloud Sync screen displays an Unlock button and the localized price. Tap Unlock to present Apple's in-app purchase confirmation sheet.
5. Restore Purchases is available on the same screen. If this Apple Account already owns the product, the purchase is replaced by the iCloud Sync toggle and status.

The purchase uses StoreKit 2 and is not gated on the device's iCloud sign-in, storefront, clipboard contents, or device type. No Copied account is required. The same product ID is used by the iOS and Mac App Store apps.

GUIDELINE 3.1.1 FOLLOW-UP
License-key entry and external-license redemption have been removed from the iOS app. The iOS app and both extensions no longer contain the external license store or validator. iCloud Sync unlocks only through a verified StoreKit purchase or Restore Purchases. Cached external-license unlocks from build 16 are not accepted; legitimate App Store purchases are revalidated through StoreKit. The Settings row named Open-source Licenses contains attribution text only, with no activation or redemption functionality.

After a successful purchase, force-quit and reopen Copied as instructed by the Restart required banner to activate syncing. Sync uses CloudKit's private database and requires the device's iCloud account. To verify cross-device sync, use a second device signed into the same iCloud account with the purchase restored and iCloud Sync enabled.

PRIVACY
• All clipboard data stays in the user's iCloud private container; we have zero access.
• No third-party analytics in the iOS app.
• No accounts, no logins, no data collection.

EXTENSIONS
• Share Extension — bundle ID com.magneton.copied.ShareExtension. Adds "Save to Copied" to the system share sheet.
• Action Extension — bundle ID com.magneton.copied.ClipperAction. Adds the "Copied Clipper" one-tap capture action.
